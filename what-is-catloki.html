What is CATLOKI (CLOKI)?

Last updated: November 9, 2026

CATLOKI (ticker CLOKI) is a community meme coin on the Solana blockchain, led by a fearless orange cat in a Viking helmet. It is a meme coin: it does not promise utility, returns or a roadmap, and nothing on this site should be read as a promise of any kind.

What do “revoked authority” and “immutable metadata” mean?

The mint authority is the right to create new tokens. When it is revoked, nobody can mint more CLOKI. The freeze authority is the right to freeze token accounts. When it is revoked, nobody can freeze your tokens. Immutable metadata means the token’s name and symbol cannot be changed. These are on-chain facts you can check on Solscan. They do not tell you anything about price, liquidity or future performance.

What is a meme coin?

A meme coin is a token created mainly for fun and community, not for a product or a promise. Its value depends on attention and trading activity, which can change quickly. CATLOKI is a meme coin: no utility, returns or roadmap are promised.
