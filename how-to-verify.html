How to verify the CATLOKI contract address

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Many tokens copy the name and the image of popular meme coins. The only reliable identifier of a Solana token is its mint address. Here is how to check that you are looking at the right one.

The CATLOKI mint address

9YJN9DVpujzEA2smVVLeS5PA7Kkz67yA3JAHwFuNX91K

Solana addresses are case-sensitive. A single changed letter or a lowercase/uppercase mix-up is a different token.

Step by step

  1. Copy the mint address from this website (or from the home page with the copy button), not from a message, a reply or a direct message.
  2. Open the token page on Solscan and compare the address at the top with the one above, character by character.
  3. On the same page, check the authorities: the mint authority and the freeze authority should show as revoked, and the metadata should be immutable. As read on 24 October 2026, they are.
  4. Check that the token uses the standard SPL Token program (not Token-2022).
  5. Before you confirm a swap in your wallet or on Jupiter, make sure the output token address is exactly the one above. Shortened addresses show the first and last characters: 9YJN…X91K.
  6. If anything does not match, stop and do not sign the transaction.

Red flags

  • Anyone asking for your seed phrase or private key. No real team member will ever do that.
  • Links to “claim” or “airdrop” pages that ask you to connect a wallet and sign something you do not understand.
  • Another token with the name CATLOKI or the ticker CLOKI but a different mint address.
  • Direct messages offering support, giveaways or “verification” for a fee.

What verification does not tell you

Checking the contract confirms which token you are dealing with and how the token contract is configured. It does not tell you the price, the liquidity, whether the price will rise or fall, or whether the people around a project can be trusted. It is not an audit.
