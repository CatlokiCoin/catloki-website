Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Summary

This website does not ask you to create an account, does not use cookies, and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell personal data.

We do not collect personal information through catloki.com. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs. This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement, and we do not receive that data.

3. Our social media channels

We run community channels on X, Telegram, Discord and TikTok. When you interact with them, for example by following, commenting or posting, that platform processes your data under its own privacy policy. We only see what those platforms show to the account owner, such as public comments and basic statistics.

4. Tools used to publish our content

We use platform tools and APIs, including the TikTok for Developers Content Posting API, to publish our own content to our own accounts. These tools only act on the accounts we own, after we authorize them. They do not collect or store information about other users. Access tokens are kept private and are never published.

5. Links to other sites

Linked sites such as Solscan, DexScreener and the social platforms have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
