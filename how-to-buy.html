How to buy CATLOKI on Jupiter

Last updated: November 7, 2026

CATLOKI trades on Solana decentralized exchanges. This guide shows how a swap on Jupiter works. This website does not sell tokens, cannot make transactions for you and never asks you to connect a wallet.

Before you start

  • Meme coins are highly speculative and liquidity can be thin. Start with an amount you are fully prepared to lose.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key. No real team member will ask for it.
  • Read how to verify the contract first, so you know which token you are buying.

Steps

  1. Get a Solana wallet. Install a Solana wallet app or browser extension from its own website. Write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline.
  2. Add SOL. Buy SOL on an exchange you trust and withdraw it to your wallet address on the Solana network. Keep a little SOL aside for network fees.
  3. Open Jupiter. Use the button below. It opens Jupiter in a new tab with SOL as the input token and CATLOKI as the output token.
  4. Check the token address. The output token must be exactly 9YJN9DVpujzEA2smVVLeS5PA7Kkz67yA3JAHwFuNX91K. Compare it with the address on this page and on Solscan.
  5. Read the warnings. Jupiter currently shows warnings for this token, such as “Not Verified” and “Low Organic Activity”. They are there for your protection. Read them and decide for yourself.
  6. Check price impact and slippage. With limited liquidity, large swaps can move the price against you. Try a small amount first and look at the price impact before you confirm.
  7. Confirm in your wallet. Only sign a transaction you understand, then check your balance after it completes.

Common questions

Why does Jupiter say “Not Verified”?

It means the token has not been marked as verified in Jupiter’s own list. It does not say whether the token is good or bad. Treat it as a reason to double-check the mint address.

Can I buy with something other than SOL?

Jupiter lets you pick other input tokens that you hold in your wallet. The steps are the same: always check the output token address before you confirm.
